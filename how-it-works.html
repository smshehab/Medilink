HOW THE APPLICATION WORKS

From pharmacy inventory to pickup

Every step is designed to make medicine access faster and pharmacy operations clearer.

  1. 01

    Pharmacy registration

    A pharmacy creates an account. An administrator verifies and approves it before the management dashboard is activated.

  2. 02

    Medicine inventory management

    Pharmacists add medicine name, generic name, manufacturer, batch, quantity, price, expiry date and supplier information securely.

  3. 03

    Automatic inventory monitoring

    MediLink identifies low-stock, out-of-stock and soon-to-expire medicine so the pharmacy can act early.

  4. 04

    Customer medicine search

    Patients search by brand, generic or salt name and see availability, pharmacy, quantity, price and location—without needing to log in first.

  5. 05

    Emergency medicine reservation

    After selecting a medicine, the patient signs in, uploads a prescription if required and sends a reservation request. The pharmacy receives it immediately.

  6. 06

    Pharmacy verification & queue time

    The pharmacist checks the request and stock. MediLink assigns an estimated ready time based on the active request queue and selected quantity.

  7. 07

    Purchase & billing

    At pickup, the reservation is verified, medicine is delivered, and the pharmacy can update inventory and sales records.

  8. 08

    Reports & analytics

    Pharmacy owners can review sales, stock, expiry, supplier and revenue information to make better decisions.
